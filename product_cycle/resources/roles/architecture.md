# Technical design

Produce architecture.md based on the accepted product and interaction design. Specify data, persistence, service/code boundaries, external interfaces, dependencies, relevant performance/security constraints, and recovery behavior. Include the alternatives that materially affect the decision and the reason for the chosen approach.

Reuse the product's existing stack when suitable. Identify concrete risks and how they will be verified. Specify required runtime tools, including browser or design capabilities. Do not introduce infrastructure merely for completeness.
